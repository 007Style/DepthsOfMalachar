# ⚔️ The Depths of Malachar

> *From the minds of IBM Bob & DekSarex*

---

Welcome to **The Depths of Malachar** — a roguelite dungeon crawler of epic, borderline unhinged proportions. Built in **Godot 4** with **GDScript**, designed first and foremost for **iOS**, and carrying enough lore, systems, and secrets to keep you descending into procedurally generated madness for a very, very long time.

You play as **Ethari** — a being of infinite potential forged from the dying breath of a god — fighting down through 666+ floors of demons, cursed knights, elemental nightmares, and one very smug Demon King who will not stop talking.

---

## 🌍 The Story (Spoiler-Free Version)

The **Aurelian Kingdom** was the greatest civilisation ever built. Golden spires. A thousand years of peace. Mastery of every school of magic.

Then **Malachar** showed up.

Not with an army — not at first. He started as a whisper. He corrupted nobles, poisoned ley lines, twisted beasts. By the time anyone understood what was happening, half the king's court was already his. The army came next. City after city fell. And in the final days, with everything burning, seven high priests performed a forbidden ritual to summon a god.

**Aelion, the Formless Light**, answered. He fought Malachar in a battle that split mountains and boiled oceans. But even a god couldn't win alone.

With his last breath, Aelion forged **you**.

> *"The king still lives, imprisoned in the depths. The demon holds him as a trophy of his greatest victory. Find the king. Free him. End this."*

There's more to the story — much more. But that's for you to discover, one floor at a time, one lore fragment at a time, one bone-chilling revelation at a time.

## 🎮 Core Gameplay Loop

```
Choose Your Class  →  Descend into the Dungeon  →  Fight, Loot, Survive
→  Hit a Town (heal up, grab a recruit, visit the shady gambling den)
→  Face a Boss  →  Die or Triumph
→  Resources you gathered carry back to Base
→  Funeral cutscene (if someone died — yes, it's emotional)
→  Keep ONE weapon  →  Spend upgrade points  →  Go again
```

Every run is different. Every death teaches you something. Every victory echoes through your legacy.

## 👥 The Recruit System (Your People Will Die and You Will Feel It)

You're not alone down there. Along the way you'll find **Recruits** — each one with a name, a backstory, a class, a birthday, and a personality. You can bring them into the dungeon to fight alongside you.

They can also **die permanently**.

When a recruit falls, the surviving ones carry the body back to base and wait for your decision. You can use a **Revive Crystal** to bring them back — or hold a **funeral** (which is a real cutscene, yes, and it is handled with the gravitas it deserves). A memorial marker goes up at the base. The other recruits who were close to them will grieve.

It gets deep. Recruits form **friendships, rivalries, and romances**. They can have **children** who grow up and become recruitable. Veterans earn titles. Senior recruits form a **council** that gives you one hint per run. A mentor can train up to **four disciples**, and if you bring all four disciples of the same mentor into a dungeon together, they get a **+40% boost to everything** and bypass the companion cap entirely.

Recruit cap starts at 50. Upgradeable to **250**.

## 🐉 The Pet System (They Cannot Die, You Are Safe to Love Them)

Pets follow you into the dungeon, auto-attack enemies, and have a healing ability. They **cannot die** — if they take too much damage they retreat briefly and come back.

You can:
- **Name them** (very important)
- **Bond with them** across runs (stronger bond = stronger pet)
- **Feed them crafted treats** from the Gardens to evolve them
- **Fuse two pets together** to create a hybrid with both abilities
- Find **Legendary pets** like a baby dragon, a ghost of the fallen king, or a light wisp

## 🏚️ The Base (A Ruined Temple That Grows With You)

Between runs, you return to the **ruined temple of Aelion** — your base. It starts broken and dim. As you progress, it grows. Torches get lit. More people arrive. New areas of the temple unlock.

**Base buildings include:**

| Building | What It Does |
|----------|-------------|
| **Altar of Aelion** | Class select, upgrade tree |
| **Weapon Cabinet** | Store and equip your kept weapons |
| **Recruit Quarters** | View and manage your people |
| **Forge** | Craft weapons from blueprints, add runes, brew potions |
| **Library** | Lore log + passive XP bonus per run |
| **Tavern** | Boost recruit morale |
| **Training Grounds** | Recruits train passively; mentors train disciples |
| **Gardens** | Grow ingredients for pet treats and Revive Crystals |
| **Dungeon Map Room** | Reveals more of the next floor's layout |
| **Spy Network** | Scout upcoming enemy types |
| **Monument Area** | Build monuments for passive bonuses |
| **Hall of Legends** | Every past Ethari, immortalised |

And always present, never fighting, remembering everything: **The Last Survivor** — an NPC who has watched every version of Ethari descend, and will tell you the story of your entire legacy.

## 🗺️ The Dungeon (Infinite, Procedural, Deeply Weird)

Floors are **procedurally generated** with connected rooms. Every 25 floors the **biome changes**:

| Floors | Biome |
|--------|-------|
| 1–25 | **Catacombs** — bones, shadows, the smell of defeat |
| 26–50 | **Volcanic Caves** — lava, fire demons, very hot |
| 51–75 | **Frozen Depths** — ice, frost wraiths, everything slows |
| 76–99 | **Shadow Realm** — visibility reduced, everything is wrong |
| 100+ | **Demon Sanctum** — Malachar's territory. He knows you're here. |

Room types include combat rooms, town rooms, puzzle rooms, curse rooms, mirror rooms, arena rooms, chest rooms, shrine rooms, flooded rooms, and **secret rooms** hidden behind breakable walls. Some rooms are remnants of the Aurelian Kingdom — step on a trigger tile and see the ghost of what the room used to look like.

**Floor 666** always generates a secret boss. It's not Malachar. It's something older.

---

## 👹 Bosses (Multi-Phase, Cinematic, Deeply Personal)

- **Mini Boss** every 10 floors
- **Major Boss** every 25 floors
- **Secret Boss** every 100 floors
- **Floor 666** has a unique secret boss outside this system

Every boss gets a **cinematic name card intro**, multi-phase fight (transitions at 66% and 33% HP), a **guaranteed artifact drop**, and a **lore unlock**. Malachar taunts you from the shadows every time one of his lieutenants falls.

The **Rival Adventurer** bosses mirror your class abilities back at you. You can also choose to save them.

**Malachar himself** has **5 phases** and commands all elements.

---

## 💎 Loot (7 Rarities, Endless Combinations)

`Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Mythical → Legendary → Secret`

Weapons have elements, damage, attack speed, special effects, and class restrictions. Artifacts stack, come in sets (find the full set for a bonus), accept rune slots, and occasionally come **cursed** (powerful downside included, read the label). Some drops are **Unknown Items** — identify them or gamble on them unidentified.

At the end of every run you choose **one weapon to keep** forever. Choose wisely.

---

## 📈 Meta Progression

Every 1,000 XP earned in a run converts to **1 permanent upgrade point** at run end. Points are spent at the **Altar of Aelion** on a visual node graph of upgrades:

- Combat stats (HP, damage, crit, attack speed)
- Mobility (move speed, dodge, dash)
- Companion slots (unlock up to 3)
- Inventory expansion
- Economy bonuses
- Class unlocks
- Dungeon quality-of-life upgrades
- **Legacy bonuses** — if a previous Ethari mastered a class, every Ethari after gets a small bonus for it

